A Barbados sunset tour with turtles included

Most sunset cruises are just about the view. Ours gives you the full turtle and shipwreck experience first, then times the trip back so you're on the sun deck as the light turns golden over the Caribbean Sea.

How the evening goes

  1. 3:45 PM: meet the crew by the yellow lifeguard tower at Folkestone Marine Reserve. 4:00 PM: we set off. You can also be picked up by boat from your west coast beach when we can reach it.
  2. Turtle feeding: snorkel with hawksbill sea turtles while the crew feeds them.
  3. Shipwreck and reef: snorkel above the shipwreck at Folkestone, feed the fish and explore the reef.
  4. Golden hour: slide, plunge platform, then unlimited Bajan rum punch on the way back as the sun goes down.

What's included

  • Two snorkelling stops: turtle feeding, and the shipwreck and reef at Folkestone Marine Reserve
  • Use of the water slide and plunge platform
  • Life jackets and snorkelling equipment
  • Unlimited Bajan rum punch, rum mixes, whiskey, soft drinks and bottled water
  • Music on board
  • Bathroom and changing room on board

Book early: one late tour per day

We run either the 3:00 PM tour or the 4:00 PM sunset tour each day, never both. Whichever is booked first goes ahead, so if you have your heart set on sunset, book as early as you can.
