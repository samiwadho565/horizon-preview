Burial at sea & ash scattering in Barbados

A private, peaceful farewell on the calm waters off the west coast of Barbados, for you, your family and your friends, with a caring local crew.

Call us Message us privately

A quiet goodbye on the Caribbean Sea

Saying goodbye to someone you love is never easy. For many families, the sea is where their loved one felt most at peace, and a burial at sea in Barbados is a gentle, meaningful way to honour them.

We take your family out on our private boat to the calm waters off the west coast, where you can share a few words, a moment of silence or a favourite song, and release the ashes in your own way and your own time.

How the ashes are released

  • Direct release: the ashes are scattered gently onto the water
  • Biodegradable urn: a water-soluble urn is placed on the sea and dissolves naturally

What to expect

  • The boat is private, just for your family and friends (up to 40 guests)
  • We meet by the yellow lifeguard tower at Folkestone Marine Reserve, Holetown
  • Choose your time: a calm morning, the afternoon or sunset
  • A caring crew who gives you space and time
  • A bathroom on board
