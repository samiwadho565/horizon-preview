Cancellation policy

Simple and fair. Here is how cancellations and refunds work for our boat tours and private charters.

Deposits and refunds

If you paid a deposit, you can cancel more than 24 hours before your tour and get a full refund.

To cancel or change your booking, message us on WhatsApp, call +1 246-257-6527 or email info@horizonboattoursbarbados.com.

Shared tour times

The 3:00 PM tour and the 4:00 PM sunset tour don't run on the same day. If your preferred time isn't running, we'll offer you another time or date.

Pickup

Our main meeting point is the yellow lifeguard tower at Folkestone Marine Reserve & Visitor Centre, Holetown. Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your tour. Free pickup by boat from west coast beaches is offered when the boat can get there.

Children

Children must be accompanied by an adult. Children 12 and under are US$45, and children under 3 go free.
