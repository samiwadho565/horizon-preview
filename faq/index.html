Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our turtle and shipwreck snorkel tours, sunset tours and private boat charters in Barbados.

Tours & prices

US$60 per adult and US$45 for children aged 12 and under. Children under 3 go free.

All shared tours last 2 hours. Private charters start at 2 hours and can be longer.

Shared tours leave at 10:00 AM, 12:30 PM and 3:00 PM. The sunset tour runs from 4:00 to 6:00 PM.

On the tour

Two snorkel stops (turtle feeding, and the shipwreck and reef at Folkestone Marine Reserve), the water slide and plunge platform, life jackets and snorkel gear, unlimited Bajan rum punch, rum mixes, whiskey, soft drinks and bottled water, music, and a bathroom and changing room on board.

Turtle feeding is a stop on every tour and the crew knows where the turtles feed. They are wild animals, so sightings can never be guaranteed 100%, but they are very rarely missed.

Yes. Life jackets are provided for everyone, and the glass bottom lets you see the reef and marine life without getting in the water.

Food isn't included, but it can be arranged at an extra cost. Ask us about our Bajan beach BBQ.

Pickup & booking

At Folkestone Marine Reserve & Visitor Centre in Holetown, St James, on the west coast of Barbados.

Pickup by boat from west coast beaches is free when the boat can get there. It isn't always convenient, so tell us where you're staying and we'll let you know.

If you paid a deposit, cancel more than 24 hours before your tour to get a full refund.

Use our booking page to choose your tour and see the price, then send it to us on WhatsApp or by email. The crew confirms every booking personally.
