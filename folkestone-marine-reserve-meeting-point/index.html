Where exactly to meet

Look for the yellow lifeguard tower at Folkestone Marine Reserve & Visitor Centre in Holetown, St James. That's where the crew meets every group. Please be there at least 15 minutes before your tour starts.

Folkestone sits on the calm west coast, the island's most popular area for snorkelling, and it's also where our shipwreck and reef stop takes place.

Free boat pickup on the west coast

Staying at a hotel or villa on the west coast? We can pick you up by boat, free of charge, from your beach when we can get there. It isn't always convenient to pick up away from Folkestone, so tell us where you're staying when you book and we'll confirm.

Food before or after your trip

There are plenty of bars and restaurants along the west coast, including two local restaurants right at Folkestone, so it's easy to grab a bite before or after your trip.

Getting there

Folkestone is in Holetown, on the west coast road. Taxis and local buses run along the west coast, and the Visitor Centre is easy to find. Tap "Open in Google Maps" above for directions from where you're staying.
