Our Glass-Bottom Boat in Holetown

A spacious two-deck glass-bottom boat built for fun: a water slide, a plunge platform, a sun deck and a bathroom on board. Meet the boat and the crew behind Horizon Boat Tours Barbados.

Book a tour

Built for turtles, snorkelling and fun

Our boat is a glass-bottom party boat with two decks: a shaded lower deck and an open sun deck up top. Through the glass bottom you can watch the reef and marine life pass beneath you, which makes it a great choice for families with young children and anyone who'd rather stay dry.

On board

  • Glass-bottom viewing panel
  • Two decks: lower deck and sun deck
  • Water slide and plunge platform
  • Bathroom and changing room
  • Music on board
  • Life jackets and snorkelling equipment for every guest
  • Room for up to 40 guests on private charters, and a maximum of 20 on shared tours

Small groups, personal service

We specialise in intimate, personal tours. Shared trips are capped at 20 guests, even though the boat holds 40, so the crew can look after everyone, help nervous snorkellers and make sure each guest gets the full experience.

Where we're based

We operate from Folkestone Marine Reserve in Holetown, St James, right on the calm west coast of Barbados, home to some of the island's best snorkelling.
