What to bring on a boat tour in Barbados

A quick packing checklist for snorkelling, turtle and sunset boat tours on the west coast.

By the Horizon crew · Updated November 2026

Good news: on our tours, the important things are already on board, including life jackets, snorkel gear, unlimited drinks and a bathroom. Here's what to bring yourself.

The essentials

  • Swimwear (wear it under your clothes to save time)
  • A towel
  • Reef-safe sunscreen
  • Sunglasses and a hat
  • A dry change of clothes
  • Cash or card for any extras
  • Your phone in a waterproof case, or an underwater camera

Nice to have

  • A light cover-up or T-shirt for sun protection
  • Your own mask, if you prefer it (we provide gear)
  • Motion-sickness tablets, if you're prone to it
  • A small dry bag

What we provide

  • Two snorkelling stops: turtle feeding, and the shipwreck and reef at Folkestone Marine Reserve
  • Use of the water slide and plunge platform
  • Life jackets and snorkelling equipment
  • Unlimited Bajan rum punch, rum mixes, whiskey, soft drinks and bottled water
  • Music on board
  • Bathroom and changing room on board
