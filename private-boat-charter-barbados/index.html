The whole boat, just for your group

A private boat charter in Barbados with Horizon gives you everything our shared tours have, without sharing. Choose your time, bring your people, and the crew takes care of the rest, from the turtle stop to the music and the rum punch.

Private charter prices

Our pricing is simple: US$300 per hour, with a 2-hour minimum, for up to 10 guests. After that:

  • Each additional hour: US$250
  • Each additional guest (over 10): US$50 per person, per trip
  • Maximum: 40 guests, the full capacity of the boat
  • Length: from 2 hours up to 6 hours

Perfect for

  • Birthdays and anniversaries: a day on the water they'll talk about for years

Burial at sea and ash scattering

We also offer private, respectful burial at sea and ash scattering for families who want to say goodbye on the calm waters of the west coast.

What's included

  • Two snorkelling stops: turtle feeding, and the shipwreck and reef at Folkestone Marine Reserve
  • Use of the water slide and plunge platform
  • Life jackets and snorkelling equipment
  • Unlimited Bajan rum punch, rum mixes, whiskey, soft drinks and bottled water
  • Music on board
  • Bathroom and changing room on board

Hungry? There are plenty of bars and restaurants along the west coast, including two local restaurants right at Folkestone, so it's easy to grab a bite before or after your charter.
